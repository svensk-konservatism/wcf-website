Founders CircleFor American patrons

Join the Founders Circle.

The Founders Circle is the community of American patrons who found and sustain the work of Western Civilization Fund. Its members back Europe’s conservative champions: the founders and institutions defending free speech, faith, family, sovereign nations, and free enterprise. Together, they are the founding generation of the fund.

America and Europe are one civilization, and its inheritance is now contested in the lands where it began. Members of the Founders Circle stand with the Europeans building the institutions to defend it.

01Membership

Membership levels

Membership is annual and open to gifts of every size, from $100 a year. Each level includes the benefits of the levels before it.

  1. 01

    Friend

    $100 a year

    For all who wish to stand with the work from its first days.

    • The Founders Circle letter: regular reports from Europe on the founders and institutions the fund supports
    • The fund’s annual report
    • Recognition in the annual report, if you wish
    Become a Friend →
  2. 02

    Associate

    $250 a year

    For those who want to deepen their understanding of Europe.

    Everything at the Friend level, and:

    • Early access to the annual report and to new country profiles from Europe
    Become an Associate →
  3. 03

    Patron

    $1,000 a year

    For those who want to follow the work closely.

    Everything at the Associate level, and:

    • An invitation to the annual Founders Circle call with the fund’s leadership
    Become a Patron →
  4. 04

    Steward

    $5,000 a year

    For those who want to help carry the work forward.

    Everything at the Patron level, and:

    • An invitation to the annual Founders Circle gathering
    • Briefings during the year on developments in Europe
    Become a Steward →
  5. 05

    Guardian

    $10,000 a year

    For those committed to building lasting institutions.

    Everything at the Steward level, and:

    • A yearly private briefing with the fund’s leadership
    Become a Guardian →
  6. 06

    Benefactor

    $25,000 a year

    For those who want to know the work and the people behind it.

    Everything at the Guardian level, and:

    • Meetings with members of the Advisory Board
    • Opportunities to meet grantees, in Europe or in the United States
    Become a Benefactor →
  7. 07

    Cornerstone

    $50,000 and above, by conversation

    For those who wish to stand at the foundation of the fund.

    Everything at the Benefactor level, and:

    • A personal relationship with the fund’s leadership
    • An invitation to visit grantees in Europe, at your own expense
    Talk to us about Cornerstone →

Benefits are offered in thanks and are kept deliberately modest. Grants are made at the sole discretion of Western Civilization Fund: membership carries no role in grant decisions, and gifts cannot be designated for a particular grantee.

04By arrangement

Other ways to give

Every arrangement below begins with a conversation. Write to us at andreas.enevald@gmail.com

  1. 01

    Donor-advised fund

    You may recommend a grant to Western Civilization Fund from your donor-advised fund. Write to us for the details your sponsor will need.

  2. 02

    Family foundation

    Private and family foundations may make grants to the fund as a public charity. We will gladly provide whatever your foundation requires.

  3. 03

    Stock and cryptocurrency

    Gifts of appreciated stock or cryptocurrency can be arranged. Please contact us first.

  4. 04

    Corporate gifts

    Companies may give directly or match the gifts of their employees. Contact us to arrange.

Important information

Western Civilization Fund is a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity. Contributions are charitable donations, tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and do not constitute an investment or carry any financial return.

Grants are made at the sole discretion of Western Civilization Fund. Members and donors have no role in grant decisions, and gifts cannot be designated for a particular grantee.

Where a member receives goods or services in return for a gift, we will tell you their estimated value, which may reduce the deductible amount.

Western Civilization Fund is independent and is not affiliated with, or acting on behalf of, any government. No part of any gift is used to support or oppose any political party or candidate, or in any election campaign.
