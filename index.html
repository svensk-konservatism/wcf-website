01The stakes

One civilization, one future.

America and Europe are bound together by more than geography or interest: by a common culture and faith, by ties of family, and by generations of standing together in war and in peace.

Above all, we share one civilizational inheritance. It runs from Jerusalem, Athens, and Rome, through the Christian centuries and the English common law, to the American founding. It taught the world that every person holds rights from the Creator that no government may deny, that power must answer to law, and that free nations govern themselves.

These principles belong to no single continent. They will be defended together on both sides of the Atlantic, or they will be lost on both.

02What is at risk

The foundations are under pressure.

Across Europe, the principles that built the West are being steadily worn away, and the very authorities meant to serve citizens are too often turned against them. The pressures are familiar to every American conservative.

  • Censorship

    Speech codes, online monitoring, and penalties for lawful opinion are narrowing what citizens may say.

  • Hostility to faith

    Christian faith is being pushed to the margins of public life, believers have been arrested for silent prayer, and Europe’s Judeo-Christian heritage is fading from its schools.

  • Family decline

    Marriage and birth rates are falling, and too few young people are taught to love the civilization they inherit.

  • Lost sovereignty

    Mass immigration and EU overreach are weakening secure borders and the right of nations to govern themselves, as power passes to bodies that answer to no voter.

  • Big government

    High taxes, heavy regulation, and a growing state are stifling enterprise, innovation, and investment.

None of this is inevitable. The answer is not to abandon our shared heritage but to renew it, through institutions strong enough to carry it forward.

03The case

Why American support matters.

Europe’s course bears directly on America. A Europe unsure of its own heritage is less able to hold firm against threats from without and decay from within, and speech rules written there increasingly reach American citizens and companies.

American conservatives have built the institutions that sustain a movement: research centers that inform public debate, legal advocates who defend liberty in the courts, independent media, and programs that train future leaders.

Europe’s conservatives are our civilizational allies. They have the conviction, the talent, and a rising generation ready to lead. Too often, they lack the institutions to sustain that work and the early support to build them.

Western Civilization Fund exists to close that gap. As a private, independent charity, we give American donors a disciplined and accountable way to stand with those defending the West in Europe.

04Who we are

Political venture capital for Europe.

We bring the discipline of venture capital to civic life. We find capable founders early, back them with focused grants, and stand behind those who prove themselves as their work grows.

Mission

To identify, fund, and scale the leaders building Europe’s conservative institutions: the founders, writers, educators, and organizers who defend free speech, faith, family, sovereign nations, and free enterprise.

Vision

A free and sovereign Europe, confident in its Judeo-Christian heritage, prosperous through free enterprise, and united with America in one civilization.

05How we work

From first grant to lasting institution.

  1. 01

    Scout

    Through our Advisory Board and our networks across Europe, we identify promising founders at the outset of their work.

  2. 02

    Seed

    We make focused first grants with clear milestones, and we decide quickly so that good work can begin.

  3. 03

    Scale

    We measure results, renew support for what works, and help proven organizations grow in reach and strength.

Our principles

Independent of party and government

Our work is educational and charitable, and our judgments are our own. We stand apart from party politics and from every government, and we take no part in elections.

People before programs

We back founders of character and conviction who have a clear plan, and we hold them to it.

Institutions, not moments

We fund organizations built to serve for decades, not short-lived campaigns.

European leadership

Our grantees know their nations, languages, and cultures. We give them the means to lead.

06What we defend

Five pillars of a free society.

  1. 01

    Free speech

    The freedom to speak, publish, and debate without fear is a natural right and the first safeguard of every other liberty.

  2. 02

    Religious liberty and Judeo-Christian heritage

    Western Civilization was built on the Judeo-Christian tradition. We defend the freedom of believers to pray, worship, and live by their faith in public, and the place of that heritage in national life.

  3. 03

    Family and the next generation

    Strong families are the foundation of a healthy nation. We support the family and the formation of the young, including a new generation of Christian leaders across the continent.

  4. 04

    Sovereign nations and secure borders

    Free peoples govern themselves. We defend national self-government against EU overreach and every nation’s right to secure its borders and determine its own future.

  5. 05

    Free enterprise and prosperity

    Prosperity is the work of free people, not of the state. We champion property rights, free markets, low taxes, and light regulation, and we oppose socialism in every form. We stand with the innovators, from industry to technology and digital assets, who create wealth and opportunity.

07What we fund

Back the champions of Western Civilization.

We seed the people and institutions that carry these principles into public life, in eight areas.

Early-stage seed grants to founders and new institutions, for charitable and educational purposes. Never parties, candidates, or campaigns.

  • 01

    Independent media and journalism

    Newsrooms, publications, podcasts, and video channels that report freely and widen the public debate.

  • 02

    Think tanks and policy institutes

    Research institutes that develop serious policy and make the case for it in public life.

  • 03

    Christian and faith-based initiatives

    Christian media, schools, and networks of young leaders that strengthen faith and its place in public life.

  • 04

    Culture and metapolitics

    Initiatives that shape the culture upstream of politics, from the arts and academia to sports and influential new voices.

  • 05

    Youth and student leadership

    Student societies, fellowships, and training programs that form the next generation of leaders.

  • 06

    Legal defense of free speech and religious liberty

    Legal organizations that defend citizens, journalists, and believers whose basic freedoms are under threat.

  • 07

    Free enterprise and entrepreneurship

    Pro-growth initiatives, from economic education to programs for founders and technologists, that make the case for free markets.

  • 08

    Technology for the conservative cause

    Tech ventures that put AI and new technology to work for free speech, faith, family, and free enterprise.
